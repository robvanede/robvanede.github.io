Feb 04

On the train

  • By Rob in Australia, Travel blog

After a pretty good night in seats that reclined very far and have fantastic legroom, this is the view on the Nullarbor Plain. Every half an hour a little bushy thing, but nothing but unending flatness for most of the time. At the horizon, you even see the curvature of the earth.
The last part of the journey was especially spectacular, as there were huge electrical storms around the train. Very dark skies an lots of lightning. The rain stopped just before arrival in Kalgoorlie, so the walk to the hostel was dry.
The hostel itself is on Kalgoorlie’s infamous Hay Street, right in the middle of the red-light district. Most occupants are mine-workers, so staying here gives a good idea about the real life in this town. For a few days it is a very interesting experience, but living here would be something else…

  • Indian Pacific, Nullarbor

Climbed Mnt. Wellington

  • By Rob in Australia, Travel blog

Walked to the top of Mount Wellington, the mountain that overlooks Hobart. The way up took about 2.5 hours, during which I was accompanied by Larry, a retired Canadian doctor who is now working as a poker instructor onboard the huge cruiseship that arrived here this morning, and a geography teacher from Hobart. Learned that, even in a small town like this, there two culturally different parts: east and west, with west (of course) being the wealthier part.
The view, as you can see, was fantastic.
The Tasman Peninsula at the back might be one of my next destinations.
The way back was a pretty rough track over huge boulders on a high plateau for most of the way.
Will probably feel my legs and feet tomorrow…

Oct 07

Paragliding Mount Bora, Manilla

  • By Rob in Australia, Paragliding, Things to do, Travel

Mount Bora, close to Manilla is a very famous paragliding location in New South Wales. This is where the Australian distance record of 335 kms was flown, and also the location of the 2007 Paragliding World Championships.

Starting locations in all wind directions and you are allowed to land everywhere, except the neighbor’s horse paddock. They advise against landing on varanos or kangaroos though.

Maybe it is worth the hassle of bringing the paragliding gear then…

  • Australia, New South Wales, Paragliding
